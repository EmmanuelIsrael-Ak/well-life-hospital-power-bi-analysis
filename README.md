# well-life-hospital-power-bi-analysis
Power BI analysis of patient admissions and clinical operations data from 2021–2024.

Well-Life General Hospital — Patient Admissions & Clinical Operations Analysis
📊 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes patient records from Well-Life General Hospital covering the period 2021–2024.

The analysis focuses on patient admissions, demographic distribution, and major medical conditions to help hospital management understand patient patterns and support data-driven decisions across hospital administration and clinical operations.

The project transforms raw patient records into an interactive Power BI dashboard, providing a clear and accessible view of the hospital's patient data.

🎯 Business Problem

Well-Life General Hospital has accumulated patient records from 2021 to 2024 but lacks a simple and interactive way to analyze the information.

Management needs clearer visibility into patient admissions, demographic distribution, and major medical conditions in order to identify important patterns and make informed operational decisions.

Without an effective analytical dashboard, extracting meaningful insights from individual patient records can be time-consuming and difficult.

🎯 Project Objectives

The analysis aims to:

Analyze patient admissions between 2021 and 2024.
Examine the distribution of patients across key demographic groups.
Identify the major medical conditions recorded.
Analyze patterns and trends in patient records over time.
Provide management with an interactive dashboard for easier data interpretation.
Generate actionable insights that can support hospital administration and clinical decision-making.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Power BI — Data analysis and visualization
Power Query — Data cleaning and transformation

📁 Dataset

The dataset contains hospital patient records covering the period 2021–2024. The analysis focuses on information relevant to patient admissions, demographics, and medical conditions.

Data Preparation

Before building the dashboard, the data was prepared and transformed using Power Query. Key preparation activities included:

Reviewing the dataset structure and data types.
Cleaning and standardizing data where required.
Handling missing or inconsistent values.
Removing unnecessary data fields.
Ensuring fields were correctly formatted for analysis.
Preparing the data for visualization and reporting in Power BI.
DAX — Measures and calculations
Microsoft Excel — Data source/preparation
